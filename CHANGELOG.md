# Changelog

All notable changes to `filament-date-input` will be documented in this file.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-16

- First release, with its tests: the `DateInput` field for Filament v5 with masked typing,
  a decade › year › month › day calendar and a help pop-up; Livewire state and saved value in `Y-m-d`; options
  `displayFormat`, `views`, `startView`, `decadesPerPage`, `minDate`, `maxDate`, `pastDate`, `firstDayOfWeek`,
  `todayButton` and `helpPopover`; English translations; script and stylesheet URLs versioned by a hash of their
  contents.
